Clipping Pipeline

Clipping Pipeline is a small personal tool that turns long videos into short vertical clips and posts them to the owner's own YouTube channel and Instagram account.

What it does

  • Cuts short clips from video the owner has permission to use.
  • Uploads finished clips to the owner's YouTube channel.
  • Temporarily stores a clip in the owner's Google Drive so Instagram can fetch it, then deletes it.
  • Reads the owner's own Google Drive folders for source footage.

The app is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
